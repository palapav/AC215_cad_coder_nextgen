In the current phase, we deploy the 2B Qwen-3-VL-Instruct model in production (to Modal Labs for MS4) because it offers a strong quality–latency–cost tradeoff and allows us to rapidly iterate on prompts, evaluation, and system integration. Our training curves and projections indicate that the 8B variant can further improve IOU and valid sample rates, but it requires a more sophisticated serving stack on Google Cloud (e.g., GKE/Vertex AI with high-memory GPUs, quantization, and streaming). Once we’ve hardened the pipeline with the 2B model, the next step is to productionize the 8B model as a higher-quality tier or ‘pro’ configuration, keeping the 2B model as a cheaper, faster baseline.
